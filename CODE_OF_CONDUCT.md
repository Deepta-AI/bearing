# Code of conduct

Bearing follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short: review the work, not the person; assume competence and good
intent; keep disagreements specific and evidence-based. Harassment,
discrimination and personal attacks are not tolerated.

Report a concern privately to the maintainer at dev@deeptaai.com.
Reports are read only by the maintainer and handled in confidence.
