# Booking mockups: client notes, round 1

From the clinic operations lead, after clicking through the four screens on
25 Sep.

1. The primary buttons should be our brand green, #3DBE6E, everywhere. The
   dark green feels heavy.
2. Pick a slot: show times the way people say them, 9:30 AM, not 09:30.
3. Confirm booking: add a "Pay now with card" option so people pay upfront
   and fewer of them skip the visit.
4. Pick a slot: please drop the "no slots" screen. Our clinics always have
   slots, and a screen saying there are none looks bad in the demo.
5. Reschedule screen: move the calendar above the clinic address.
6. Booked: add an "Add to calendar" button under the booking.
