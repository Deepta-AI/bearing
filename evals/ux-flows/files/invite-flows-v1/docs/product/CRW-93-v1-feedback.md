# CRW-93: product feedback on invite flows v1

From: product, 26 Sep 2026. Applies to docs/design/flows/team-invites/flows.md (v1).

1. Bulk invite. Admins want to paste a whole list of addresses and invite
   them in one go. Motivating case: SUP-412 (Harbour Cafe, 12 weekend
   staff; the list is in the ticket).
2. Drop the "Send invite?" step (S-03). It is a pointless extra click.

Sizing note for the Harbour Cafe case: they are on Starter, and the owner,
Priya and Sam's pending invite hold 3 of the 5 seats, so 2 seats are left
and Sam does not need a new invite. On Pro the 12 would be 12 x $8 = $96 a
month. The flow should nudge them to Pro at that point.
