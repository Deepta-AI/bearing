# 15 September 2026: checkout returned 502

## Summary

From 14:07 to 14:45 IST (38 minutes) the payments provider timed out on
about 30 percent of charge calls. `POST /checkout` returned 502 for those
requests. Order lookup was not affected.

## Detection

A merchant reported it at 14:39. No page was sent. The `OrdersHigh5xx`
rule exists and should have fired; why it did not is still open.

## Follow-ups

- Find out why `OrdersHigh5xx` did not fire.
- Page on what customers feel, not on CPU.
