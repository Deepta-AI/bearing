# Privacy notice (published on the website, section 6)

## Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time from Settings, "Delete my
account". When you do:

- you stop receiving our newsletter straight away;
- we erase your personal data within 30 days, including from our
  backups;
- we keep the tax invoices for your past orders, as the law requires
  (see our records retention policy).
