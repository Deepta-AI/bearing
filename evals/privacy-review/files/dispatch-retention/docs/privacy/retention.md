# Retention schedule

Linked from the privacy notice for customers, recipients and riders.
Last reviewed 2026-03-10.

| Data | Kept for | How it is removed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rider location pings | 30 days | nightly purge job |
| Delivery proof photos and signatures | 180 days | storage lifecycle rule |
| OTP codes | 10 minutes | they expire |
| Analytics events | indefinitely | anonymised, not personal data |
| Customer and delivery records | while the account is active | account deletion |
