# PF and ESI contributions

Updated: 2026-04-10

Ledgerleaf calculates Provident Fund and Employees' State Insurance each
month from the salary structure. Rates are set by law; the table shows the
defaults the product applies.

| Scheme | Employee share | Employer share | Wage base | Ceiling |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| EPF | 12% | 3.67% | Basic plus DA | INR 15,000 basic (optional above) |
| EPS | none | 8.33% | Basic plus DA | INR 15,000 |
| EDLI | none | 0.5% | Basic plus DA | INR 15,000 |
| ESI | 0.75% | 3.25% | Gross wages | Applies when gross is INR 21,000 or less |

## Contributing above the ceiling

By default PF is calculated on basic up to INR 15,000. To contribute on the
full basic, turn on "PF on actual basic" per employee under Compensation.
The employer share on the amount above the ceiling goes entirely to EPF.

## ESI eligibility changes

ESI eligibility is checked at the start of each contribution period (April
to September and October to March). An employee who crosses INR 21,000 in
the middle of a period stays covered until the period ends.
