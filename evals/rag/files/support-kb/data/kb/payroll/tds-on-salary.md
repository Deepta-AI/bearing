# TDS on salary

Updated: 2026-04-02

Ledgerleaf deducts TDS monthly from projected annual income under the
regime each employee declares. If an employee has not declared a regime,
the run shows error E-6001 and uses the new regime as the default for that
month.

Employees submit investment proofs from the self-service app between 1
January and 31 January. Proofs not approved by an admin by the February
run are ignored for the rest of the year, and TDS is recalculated.

Form 16 is generated from Tax > Form 16 after the admin uploads part A from
TRACES (error E-6004 if missing). Form 16 is published to employees by 15
June.
