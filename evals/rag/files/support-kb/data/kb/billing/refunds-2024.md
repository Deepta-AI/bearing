# Refund policy (2024)

> Superseded on 2026-06-01 by "Refunds and cancellations". Kept for
> customers whose annual plan was bought before that date.

Updated: 2024-03-18

Annual plans can be cancelled within 30 days of purchase for a full refund.
After 30 days, annual plans are not refundable, but you can switch to a
monthly plan at renewal. Monthly plans are not refunded for part months.
Refunds reach the original payment method in 5 to 7 working days.
