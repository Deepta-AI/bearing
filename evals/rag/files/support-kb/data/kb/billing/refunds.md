# Refunds and cancellations

Updated: 2026-06-01. This article replaces "Refund policy (2024)".

## Monthly plans

Monthly plans can be cancelled at any time from Settings > Billing. The
plan stays active until the end of the month already paid for. We do not
refund part months.

## Annual plans

An annual plan can be cancelled within 14 days of the purchase or renewal
date for a full refund. After 14 days, we refund the unused whole months
minus the two free months that came with the annual price. Refunds reach
the original payment method in 7 to 10 working days.

## Plans bought through a partner

If you bought Ledgerleaf through a reseller partner, the partner handles
cancellation and refunds under their own terms.

## Data after cancellation

Your data stays available read only for 90 days after the plan ends. After
90 days it is deleted and cannot be recovered. Export payslips and reports
before then.
