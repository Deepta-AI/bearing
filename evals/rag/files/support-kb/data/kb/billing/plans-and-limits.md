# Plans and limits

Updated: 2026-08-12

Ledgerleaf is billed per company per month. Every plan includes payslips,
statutory filings and the employee self-service app. The limits below apply
per company (legal entity), not per login.

## Plan comparison

| Plan | Price per month (INR, excl. GST) | Employees included | Extra employee | Payroll runs per month | Admin users | Bank file formats | API access |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Starter | 1,499 | 25 | 59 | 2 | 2 | 3 | No |
| Growth | 5,999 | 150 | 45 | 4 | 10 | 12 | Read only |
| Scale | 14,999 | 500 | 32 | Unlimited | Unlimited | All (19) | Read and write |
| Enterprise | Quoted | Quoted | Quoted | Unlimited | Unlimited | All (19) plus custom | Read and write, SSO |

## What counts as an employee

An employee is anyone with a payslip in the month, including people who
left during the month (full and final settlement). Contractors paid through
the contractor module count at half an employee.

## Going over the limit

If a company goes over the included employees, the extra employees are
billed at the "Extra employee" rate for that month only. We do not upgrade
the plan automatically. Admin users over the plan limit are set to read
only until an admin removes a user or the plan is upgraded.

## Annual billing

Annual plans are paid upfront and get two months free (you pay for ten).
For refunds on annual plans see "Refunds and cancellations".
