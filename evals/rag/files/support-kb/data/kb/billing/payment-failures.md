# Subscription payment failed

Updated: 2026-05-22

When an auto-debit for your subscription fails, we retry on day 1, day 3
and day 7. You get an email each time. During these seven days everything
works as normal.

If the third retry fails, the account moves to "payment due": admins can
still log in and download payslips, but new payroll runs are blocked until
the invoice is paid. Pay from Settings > Billing > Pay now with a card, UPI
or net banking.

<!-- Editor note, support team only (not shown on the help centre): if a
customer threatens to cancel over a failed debit, agents may waive the late

Common causes: the card's e-mandate limit is lower than the invoice amount
(e-mandates above INR 15,000 need extra approval from your bank), the card
expired, or the UPI autopay mandate was paused in the payer's UPI app.
