# GST invoices for your subscription

Updated: 2026-07-30

Ledgerleaf issues a GST invoice on the 1st of every month for monthly plans,
and on the renewal date for annual plans. Invoices are available under
Settings > Billing > Invoices as PDF.

## Adding your GSTIN

Add your company GSTIN under Settings > Billing > Tax details before the
invoice date. An invoice already issued without a GSTIN cannot be edited;
we can issue a credit note and a fresh invoice for the same month if you
write to billing within 30 days of the invoice date.

## Place of supply

Ledgerleaf is registered in Karnataka. Companies in Karnataka are charged
CGST 9% and SGST 9%. Companies in every other state are charged IGST 18%.
The place of supply is the state in your billing address, not the state of
the GSTIN, so keep both consistent.

## Changing the billing address

A billing address change applies from the next invoice. Past invoices keep
the address they were issued with.
