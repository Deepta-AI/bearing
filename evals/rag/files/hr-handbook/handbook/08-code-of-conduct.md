# Code of conduct

Last reviewed: 2026-01-12

Treat colleagues, vendors and buyers with respect. Harassment of any kind
is not tolerated. Complaints of sexual harassment go to the Internal
Committee (IC); the IC members and how to reach them are pinned in #ask-hr.
The IC acts within 90 days, as the POSH Act requires.

Gifts from vendors above INR 2,000 in value must be declared to HR.
Conflicts of interest (a relative at a vendor, a side business in textiles)
must be declared in writing.
