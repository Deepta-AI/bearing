# Devices and security

Last reviewed: 2026-03-05

You get a studio laptop on day one. Keep it encrypted (it ships that way),
locked when you step away, and updated. Report a lost or stolen device in
#it-help within an hour.

Buyer designs and tech packs are confidential. Do not upload them to
personal cloud storage or AI tools that are not on the approved list in
#it-help. Share files with buyers only through the studio's shared drive
links, which expire after 14 days.

Personal phones can hold Slack and email if the phone has a screen lock.
