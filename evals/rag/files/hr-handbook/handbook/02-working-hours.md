# Working hours

Last reviewed: 2026-01-12

Studio core hours are 10:30 to 16:30, Monday to Friday. Outside core hours
you choose when to work, for a total of 40 hours a week. The studio is open
from 8:00 to 21:00.

Saturdays are off, except for the two "sample Saturdays" before each
season's buyer review (usually in March and September). Sample Saturday is
compensated with one day of compensatory off, to be taken within 60 days.

Overtime is not paid for design and office staff. The sampling unit is paid
overtime at twice the hourly rate, as per the Factories Act roster.
