# Holidays 2026

Published: 2025-12-15

The studio is closed on these ten fixed holidays. In addition, you choose
two restricted holidays from the list below and apply for them like leave.

| Date | Day | Holiday |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 January | Thursday | New Year's Day |
| 14 January | Wednesday | Makar Sankranti |
| 26 January | Monday | Republic Day |
| 19 March | Thursday | Ugadi |
| 1 May | Friday | May Day |
| 15 August | Saturday | Independence Day |
| 14 September | Monday | Ganesh Chaturthi |
| 2 October | Friday | Gandhi Jayanti |
| 20 October | Tuesday | Vijayadashami |
| 9 November | Monday | Deepavali |

Restricted holidays (choose two): Holi (4 March), Eid al-Fitr (21 March),
Good Friday (3 April), Bakrid (27 May), Onam (26 August), Christmas (25
December).

When a fixed holiday falls on a Saturday, there is no substitute day off.
