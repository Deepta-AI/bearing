# Welcome to Northwind Loom

Last reviewed: 2026-01-12 by the HR lead

This handbook explains how we work: hours, leave, holidays, expenses,
remote work, devices and conduct. If something here is unclear or out of
date, tell HR in #ask-hr. Where this handbook and your appointment letter
differ, your appointment letter wins.

Northwind Loom has one studio, in Bengaluru, and a small sampling unit in
Tiruppur. Studio staff follow the studio hours below; the sampling unit
has its own shift roster, posted on the unit notice board.

Your first week: you get a laptop on day one, a buddy from your team, and a
30-minute session with HR on day three to set up payroll, PF and insurance.
