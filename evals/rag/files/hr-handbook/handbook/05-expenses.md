# Expenses and reimbursements

Last reviewed: 2026-02-20

Claim expenses in the HR app within 30 days, with a photo of the bill.
Claims are paid with the next salary after your lead approves them.

| Expense | Limit | Needs pre-approval |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Local travel for work (cab, auto) | Actuals | No |
| Outstation travel (train 2AC or economy flight) | Actuals | Yes, from your lead |
| Hotel, outstation | INR 4,500 per night | Yes |
| Meals, outstation | INR 900 per day | No |
| Internet at home | INR 1,000 per month | No |
| Books and courses | INR 15,000 per year | Yes, for single items over INR 3,000 |
| Client entertainment | Actuals | Yes, from a director |

Alcohol is never reimbursed. Lost bills: a signed self-declaration covers up
to INR 500 per claim, twice a year.

Medical costs are covered by the group health insurance (INR 5 lakh family
floater, card issued in your first month), not by expense claims.
