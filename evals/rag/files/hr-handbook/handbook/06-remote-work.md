# Remote work

Last reviewed: 2026-01-12

Design and office staff can work remotely up to two days a week, agreed
with the team lead. Buyer review weeks and sample Saturdays are in the
studio for everyone.

Working from outside India needs written approval from HR at least 30 days
before, and is limited to 20 working days a year because of tax and
insurance rules.

Remote days follow core hours. Keep Slack status up to date. Staff who
work remotely can claim home internet up to INR 1,500 a month.
