# Leave policy

Effective: 2026-04-01 (replaces the 2024 policy)

The leave year runs April to March. Leave is credited monthly.

| Leave type | Days per year | Credited | Carry forward | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Annual leave | 24 | 2 per month | Up to 12 days | Apply 7 days ahead for 3 or more days |
| Sick leave | 12 | 1 per month | No | Doctor's note for 3 or more consecutive days |
| Casual leave | 6 | 6 on 1 April | No | Not more than 2 days at a time |
| Parental leave (birth parent) | 182 (26 weeks) | On request | Not applicable | As per the Maternity Benefit Act |
| Parental leave (other parent) | 15 | On request | Not applicable | Within 6 months of the birth or adoption |
| Bereavement leave | 5 | On request | Not applicable | Immediate family |

Unused annual leave above the 12-day carry-forward cap lapses on 31 March.
It is not encashed, except at exit, when up to 30 days of accrued annual
leave are paid out at basic salary.

Leave is applied in the HR app and approved by your team lead. HR sees it
only after approval.
