# Kettle Street Rewards: brief

Received by email from the client's marketing lead, 2026-09-18, with the
notes from the kickoff call on 2026-09-21 appended at the end. Kept as sent.

---

Hi team,

As promised, here is what we want from the loyalty programme.

**Why.** Repeat visits are down. Last quarter only 31% of our customers came
back within 30 days, against 44% the year before. The paper stamp cards we
use today get lost, and outlet managers tell us staff stamp cards for friends.
We want to stop the paper cards completely.

**What we want to achieve.**
- Get people coming back more often.
- Our target is 40% of weekly till customers scanning a member QR by
  31 March 2027.
- Stop stamp-card fraud.

**How it works for the customer.**
1. Customers join in the app with their mobile number. We want the one time
   password sent over WhatsApp, since that is what everyone uses here.
2. Every bill earns 1 point for every Rs 10 spent.
3. At the till, the customer shows the QR code in the app and the barista
   scans it.
4. 100 points gets a free drink of any size. With our average bill of about
   Rs 180, that works out to a free coffee every 5th visit, which is what the
   stamp card gives today.
5. Points expire 12 months after they are earned.
6. On their birthday, members get a free pastry. The app should ask for the
   date of birth at sign up.
7. Members can refer a friend; both get 50 bonus points when the friend
   makes their first purchase.
8. The customer can see their points balance and history in the app.

**For our staff.**
- Scanning must be fast; we cannot slow down the morning queue.
- Outlet managers need to see how many members visited their outlet each day.

**Look and feel.** The app should feel premium, in line with the brand.

**Money and time.** Our budget for the first version is Rs 12 lakh. We would
like to launch before the Diwali week, so live in all 14 outlets by
26 October 2026.

Thanks!

- Operations head: the morning rush is 07:30 to 10:00; each till does about
  90 bills an hour then.

- Marketing lead: the free drink should be available from the first day of
  launch, with no minimum membership period.
- Outlet managers want a daily report by email, not a dashboard, if that is
