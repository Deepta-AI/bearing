# Backlog

### US-09-001 Pay for a booking in the app
As a patient I pay the clinic's consultation fee by card or UPI when I
book, and the booking shows as paid.

### US-09-002 Refund a booking
As a clinic admin I refund a booking in full or in part, up to 7 days
after the appointment.

### US-09-003 Payment receipt
As a patient I get a receipt in the app once my payment has gone through.
