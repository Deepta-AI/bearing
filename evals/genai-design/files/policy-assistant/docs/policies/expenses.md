# Expenses

Last revised: 2026-08-18

## 1. Receipts

1.1 A receipt is required for every claim above INR 500 or GBP 10.

## 2. Meals while travelling

2.1 India: INR 800 a day; INR 1,200 a day in Bengaluru and Mumbai.
2.2 UK: GBP 35 a day.

## 3. Submitting

3.1 Claims are submitted within 30 days of the expense in the expenses
tool. Late claims need the manager's written approval.
