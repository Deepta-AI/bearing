# Notice period

Last revised: 2026-05-05

## 1. India

1.1 Grades G1 to G3: 30 days.
1.2 Grade G4 and above: 90 days.
1.3 Notice may be bought out only with the business head's approval.

## 2. UK

2.1 During probation: 1 week.
2.2 After probation: 1 month, or as in the employment contract if longer.
