# Parental leave

Last revised: 2026-07-21

## 1. India

1.1 Maternity leave: 26 weeks paid, as in the Maternity Benefit Act.
1.2 Paternity leave: 15 working days paid, taken within 6 months of the
birth or adoption.

## 2. UK

2.1 Maternity: 52 weeks; the first 16 weeks at full pay, then statutory
pay.
2.2 Paternity: 2 weeks at full pay.
2.3 Shared parental leave: eligible parents may share up to 50 weeks;
the company tops up to full pay for the first 6 shared weeks.
