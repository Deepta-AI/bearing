# Leave policy

Last revised: 2026-09-01

## 1. Annual leave

1.1 India: 21 days of earned leave a calendar year, accrued monthly.
1.2 UK: 25 days a year plus UK bank holidays.
1.3 Carry-over. India: up to 15 days carry into the next year. UK: up to
8 days carry into the next year and must be used by 31 March.

## 2. Sick leave

2.1 India: 12 days a year; a medical certificate is needed for more than
2 consecutive days.
2.2 UK: statutory sick pay rules apply; company sick pay for 10 days a
year at full pay.

## 3. Holidays

3.1 India: 10 fixed holidays and 2 optional holidays chosen from the
regional list published each December.
3.2 UK: bank holidays in England and Wales.

## 4. Leave balance

Your current balance is shown in the HR system. HR does not give
balances by email.
