# Brief: HR policy answers in Slack

From: Chief People Officer's office
Date: 2026-09-15

Leadership has asked for an assistant in Slack that answers employees'
HR policy questions so fewer of them email the HR helpdesk. The
suggestion from the leadership offsite was to "fine-tune a model on our
policies" so it knows them.

What we know:

- The helpdesk gets about 350 questions a working day, 22 working days a
  month. Two HR officers spend most of their time on them.
- Employees are in India (about 1,700) and the UK (about 700). Leave,
  holidays, notice periods and parental leave differ by country.
- The policies are revised often; see `docs/policies/CHANGELOG.md`.
- An answer should arrive in Slack within 10 seconds.
- Every answer must say which policy and section it comes from. A wrong
  answer about leave entitlement or notice period is a compliance issue.

Success: fewer tickets to the HR helpdesk.

No budget has been set yet; the CFO wants a monthly cost estimate before
approving anything.
