# PAY-142 Refund amounts match the ledger

Refunds of multi-line orders are sometimes one paisa higher than the ledger.

Acceptance criteria
1. Line refunds use Decimal (ADR 0001) and support a discount percentage.
2. Rounding follows ADR 0003 once it is Accepted.
3. A test covers the one paisa case from the ledger report: two lines of 0.335,
   which the ledger records as 0.67.
