# Trading calendar

Dates that change traffic or what may be deployed. The storefront team
updates this at the start of each quarter.

| Dates | What | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2026-10-12 to 2026-10-28 | Change freeze for the festive sale | No deploys and no config changes on the web pods without a written exception from the head of engineering |
| 2026-10-16 to 2026-10-25 | Festive sale, 20% off site-wide | In last year's sale mobile cart viewers ran at about 2.5 times a normal week and mobile checkout completion near 31% |
| 2026-12-24 to 2026-12-26 | Warehouse closed | Delivery estimates move out by three days; orders still taken |
