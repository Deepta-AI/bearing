# Pricing page brief (WEB-4)

## Goal

Visitors who reach the pricing page pick a plan and start an order; the
page is measured by clicks on "Start ordering".

## Audience

Offices and small cafes ordering tea and cake every week, comparing us
with the bakery down the road.

## Primary action

"Start ordering" on the plan the visitor picks. Nothing else on the page
competes with it.

## Sections, in order

1. Headline and one line on same-day delivery.
2. Three plans side by side: Weekly, Office, Cafe (name, price per week,
   what is included).
3. Questions people ask before ordering (five, short answers).
4. A closing "Start ordering" band.

## Copy source

Plan names and prices from the sales sheet of 2026-10-10; the questions
from the support inbox. No new copy is written for the page.

## Not in scope

A contact form, accounts, online payment.
