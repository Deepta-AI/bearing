# Backlog

Roles: admin, doctor, receptionist. Every staff member belongs to one
clinic and must never see another clinic's data.

### US-02-001 View an appointment
As any staff member of the clinic I open an appointment to see the
patient, doctor and time.

### US-02-002 Cancel an appointment
As a receptionist or clinic admin I cancel an appointment when the
patient calls. Doctors do not cancel; they ask reception.

### US-02-003 Read clinical notes
As a doctor I read the clinical notes on an appointment. Admins and
receptionists never see clinical notes (medical confidentiality).

### US-02-004 View patient details
As any staff member of the clinic I look up a patient's contact details.

### US-02-005 Patient visit history
As any staff member of the clinic I see a patient's past and upcoming
appointments (date, doctor, cancelled or not) when the patient calls.

### US-05-001 Export appointments
As a clinic admin I download my clinic's appointments as CSV for the
monthly review.
