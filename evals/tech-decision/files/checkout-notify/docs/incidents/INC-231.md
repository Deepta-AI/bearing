# INC-231: checkout slow during MSG91 degradation

Date: 2026-09-12
Severity: 2

## What happened

From 19:40 to 20:25 IST MSG91 answered in 3 to 4 seconds. Checkout sends
the SMS inside the request (internal/checkout/handler.go), so checkout
p95 went from 610 ms to 4.2 s and 6% of checkouts timed out at the load
balancer after the card was already charged.

## Follow-up

- Take SMS and email out of the request path (owner: checkout team).
- Rahul (from the review): move SMS and email to Kafka. Kafka has
  exactly-once delivery, so this would also stop duplicate SMS (PRD 4.2).
