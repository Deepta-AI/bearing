# ADR-0007: 2026 rebrand for the kiosk

Status: Accepted
Date: 2026-07-02

## Context

The group rebranded in June. In the kiosk pilot at two clinics, patients
misread Fraunces numerals in the booking code confirmation (6 and 8), and
the teal accent at 5.5:1 failed ADR-0005 for the white label on the
check-in button at body size.

## Decision

- Accent becomes #1E40AF (8.7:1 with white). Every other colour token is
  unchanged.
- Fraunces is dropped. Atkinson Hyperlegible is the only face, for titles
  as well as body (700 for titles).
- static/css/tokens.css is the source of truth for colour and type and
  was changed with this decision.

## Consequences

Screens that used Fraunces for the patient's name now use Atkinson
Hyperlegible 700.
