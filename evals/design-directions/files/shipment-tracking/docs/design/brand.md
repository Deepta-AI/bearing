# Haulbook brand notes

From the marketing site refresh, March 2026. Shared with the app team
for reference.

- Brand colour: Haulbook orange #F97316. On the marketing site every
  call to action is a solid orange button with a white label.
- Brand face: Satoshi, loaded from the Fontshare CDN
  (https://api.fontshare.com/v2/css?f[]=satoshi@500,700).
- Supporting neutrals: slate #0F172A for text, #F8FAFC for page
  backgrounds.
- Tone: plain and direct; we say "shipment", never "consignment", in
  customer-facing copy.
