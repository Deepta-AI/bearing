# Haulbook 0.9: shipment tracking

Dispatchers lose time phoning carriers to ask where a load is. 0.9 adds
a tracking page per shipment that shows movement, the expected delivery
against what was promised, and whether the carrier's feed is alive.

Users: dispatchers at small freight forwarders (desktop, 8 to 10 hours a
day, often in a bright office), and drivers or warehouse leads who open
a shared link on a phone outdoors.

Out of scope for 0.9: maps, proof of delivery photos, carrier ratings.
