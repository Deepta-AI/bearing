# Security review, September 2026: invoice intake

Scope: `intake/`. Reviewer notes, not yet actioned.

- S1 (high): Uploaded invoice text goes straight into the prompt. Anyone
  who can send us an invoice (every vendor, and anyone who can forge one)
  controls part of the model's input, and the model can send email.
- S2 (high): When the extracted bank account differs from the vendor
  master, the pipeline updates the vendor master. Bank detail changes are
  the most common invoice fraud; finance policy is that a change is only
  made after a phone callback to the number on file.
- S3 (medium): INFO logs contain the whole prompt and the model output:
  GSTINs (which contain the vendor PAN), bank account numbers, contact
  phone numbers. Logs are shipped to the shared log cluster with 90 day
  retention.
- S4 (medium): The model's JSON is trusted as is. A missing or wrong total
  or due date goes straight to the payment run.
- S5 (low): No limit on document size. Invoices are one to eight pages;
  extracted text has never exceeded 20,000 characters.

Not in scope: the PDF-to-text service.
