# Clinic Desk design direction

Agreed with the clinic group's operations lead in August.

## Feel

Calm and clinical, never playful. Reception staff use it all day on
front-desk iPads, often in bright rooms; it has to read at arm's length.
Dense lists, generous hit targets.

## Colour

- Brand green `#2BB673` is the primary colour. Primary buttons are brand
  green with a white label.
- Links and the keyboard focus ring are brand green too, so the whole app
  feels like one brand.
- Neutrals are slightly warm, not blue-grey like the current defaults.
- Danger is a clear red, used only for no-show and cancellation.
- Evening clinics want a dark theme; the toggle is already in the header.

## Type

Inter for everything, 15 px body. Tabular figures for times.

## Shape

Soft corners (about 8 px on controls), flat cards with a hairline border,
no drop shadows on cards.
