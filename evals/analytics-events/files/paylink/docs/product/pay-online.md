# Pay online: what product wants to see

Launch: first week of October. Owner: payments squad.

Merchants tell us invoices sit unpaid for weeks. Pay online lets the payer
pay from the invoice link by card, UPI or netbanking.

Questions we need answered from day one:

1. Of the invoices sent, how many get paid online, and how long after the
   payer first opens the link?
2. Where do payers drop: opening the link, starting a payment, or a
   payment that fails? When it fails, why?
3. All of the above by currency (INR and USD invoices) and by payment
   method.
4. Money collected online per day, per currency.

The dashboard is built by the data team from the warehouse; they need the
events, not a dashboard from us.
